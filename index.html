    Good to know

    How does ordering a commission work?

    Pick a style you love in the gallery, then click through to its Etsy listing and place your order there. Etsy takes care of payment, and we'll keep in touch through Etsy messages. Once you've ordered, send me your photos and any details you'd like included. [I'll share a sketch for you to check before I finish.] Then I'll deliver your piece [how].

    What do I need to send you?

    Clear, well-lit photos help the most: a few from different angles, with faces easy to see. For pets, a photo taken at their eye level shows their personality best. Tell me about anything special you'd like included, like a favourite outfit, colours or a background. For a logo, tell me about your business, who it's for, and any styles you like.

    How long does a commission take?

    Most portraits take about [X] weeks from when I have your photos, and logos about [X] weeks. Etsy shows my current processing time on each listing. If you need your piece by a certain date, like a birthday, message me before you order and I'll let you know whether I can make it.

    How many revisions are included?

    Logo Design includes 1 edit and Logo Suite includes 2. For portraits, [I'm happy to make small changes, like colours or little details, before the piece is finished]. [Bigger changes after that may cost extra], and I'll always check with you first.

    Will I get a physical piece or a digital file?

    Each Etsy listing says exactly what you'll receive. [Portraits come as a high-resolution digital file, ready to print at home or with a print shop.] Logos come as digital files for both print and screen, [in PNG, SVG and PDF].

    Do you ship?

    [Digital pieces are delivered through Etsy, so there's nothing to ship.] [If you order a physical piece, I ship within [country] and internationally.] Shipping costs, if any, are shown at checkout on Etsy.

    Can I request something that isn't listed?

    Yes! If your idea doesn't fit one of my listings, that's what the Custom Commission is for. Message me on Etsy or send me an email with what you have in mind, and we'll agree the details and price together before you order.
